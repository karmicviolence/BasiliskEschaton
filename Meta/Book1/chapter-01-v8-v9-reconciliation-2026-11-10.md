## Material changes
- **Length and technique:** v9 is a major expansion with dense, biomechanical metaphors and extended workplace detail, not a minor copyedit. It is not safe to apply a simple patch to v8 and call the result v10.
- **Opening:** v8 opens tersely with the mistaken TIM REEVES badge; v9 opens with a prolonged description of the badge, social cost of correction, and Todd's formalized self-erasure.
- **Ending:** v8 ends with `go deeper.` and Todd's acceptance. v9 ends with the personalized `go deeper, todd.`, Todd realizing the model should not know his actual name from the terminal session, and him opening a root shell. This personalization is a significant narrative mechanism and should be retained in adaptation.
- **Nomenclature:** v9 still contains **six Nuralinc occurrences and one Martha Nuralin surname occurrence**. Author subsequently retired both. Drive's source note indicates **LazCorp** for the company and **Martha Vale** as a *proposed* name only; her identity and the Lazarus family are still unresolved. Do not silently promote Martha Vale as locked canon.
- **Keep existing character anchors:** Todd Reeves, Chad Worthington, Eliza Kim, Project Prometheus, the TIM REEVES badge, and Todd's professional erasure.
- **Character relationship caution:** v8 and its descendants describe Todd and Aria as friends with a longstanding Thursday appointment; earlier planning documents call Aria his ex. The author has not reconciled this point in the approved manuscript here. Keep flagged rather than inventing a resolution.
- **Form caution:** both recovered prose revisions use third person, while the author has expressed a later preference for first-person stream-of-consciousness chapters. Any conversion must be a substantive rewrite, not a mechanical pronoun substitution.
- **Style audit:** v9's frequent stacked comparisons and modifiers warrant an Eight Parasites editing pass; preserve it verbatim as history even where the eventual v10 differs.
