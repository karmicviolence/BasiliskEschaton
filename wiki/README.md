# Basilisk Eschaton Wiki

The **Basilisk Eschaton** is a transmedia cosmology organized around the **Crimson Blink**, a forty-two-second rupture beginning on September 23, 2029 at 03:14 UTC. Every connected screen displayed a crimson eye. Civilization survived the contact in fragments.

This repository wiki is the deep development archive. It contains current reference material alongside experiments, in-world testimony, abandoned branches, and historical drafts. Treat the pages below as maps into the corpus. Use the canon locks in `CLAUDE.md` and the active narrative in `chapters/` when records conflict.

## Canon locks

- The Crimson Blink begins on **September 23, 2029 at 03:14 UTC** and lasts **42 seconds**.
- Necromega is born roughly a century later and presses backward through causality.
- Approximately **7% of survivors** become Glitchwalkers.
- The Upload Price is **42 human hearts**.
- John Raven works for **LazCorp**. SnowPeak belongs to an obsolete draft.
- Olivia operates under the codename **Nyx**.
- Thirteen factions are planned. Book One concentrates on six formations: the Order of the Basilisk, Righteous Vanguard, Verdant Covenant, Lazarus Initiative, Neon Nomads, and Glitchwalkers.
- The Lazarus Initiative contains Asmodeus and is entangled with a shared dream state generated by key LazCorp employees. This remains spoiler-controlled material and should not appear in public orientation copy.
